## Slide 4 — Programs (pick one)

**Headline:** Pick the program that matches their pain.

**Subhead:** Lead with ONE. Each has clear economics and beats Epic-native worklists on reach and labor.

**Three cards (don't read all equally):**
- **TCM · CFO/CMO** — 2-day contact + med rec. ~$200+/patient captured. Intermountain: 41% readmission drop, $15M saved.
- **TEAM · COO/CMO** — Mandatory ~741 hospitals. 30-day surgical follow-up. Catches Day-3 flags before ~$30K readmission.
- **ED · CNO/PX** — 24–72 hr triage. ~$2/call vs ~$82 manual. Prisma: +35 pt LTR at largest ED.

**Pull quote:** "Epic records the return. We prevent it."

**Speaker note:** TCM for CFO. TEAM if mandatory CBSA. ED for front-door pain. Margaret story for ED only.
