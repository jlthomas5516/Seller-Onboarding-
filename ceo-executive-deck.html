Regulatory execution

Turn CMS mandates into automated workflows.

Three 2026 lead programs — each clears the bar: clear economic value, proven outcomes, minimal lift, strong differentiation vs. the EHR.

TCM

Transitional Care Management

Automates 2-business-day interactive contact + med rec. Secures ~$298/patient manual workflows miss. Intermountain: 41% readmission drop, $15M saved.

TEAM

Episode accountability

Mandatory 2026–2030 for ~741 hospitals. 30-day surgical follow-up, SNF transition, PCP connection. Catches Day-3 red flags before ~$30K readmission.

ED follow-up

24–72 hr automated triage. ~$2/call vs. ~$82 manual. Prevents bounce-backs; routes high-risk to live nurse. Prisma: +35 pt LTR at largest ED.
