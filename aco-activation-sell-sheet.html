The reframe

Sharper risk stratification won't move your P&L. Reaching the panel will.

Most ACOs answer a flattening savings curve by buying better analytics — a more precise list of who will cost money. But a better list doesn't change the number. In value-based care, insight you can't act on is just a more accurate way to watch savings evaporate. The constraint was never knowing who. It's reaching and changing them — at panel scale, across the affiliated and IPA lives who aren't on your EHR, in the windows that decide cost: post-discharge, rising-risk, open gaps.
